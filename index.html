Frequently Asked Questions

What type of wood does Wood Heaven use?

Wood Heaven specializes in 100% seasoned, solid Pure Sheesham (Rosewood), known for its durability, natural grain beauty and longevity in handcrafted Chinioti furniture.

Does Wood Heaven deliver furniture all over Pakistan?

Yes, we safely deliver our handcrafted furniture to all major cities across Pakistan including Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan and Peshawar with secure packaging.

Can I customize the design, carving and polish color?

Yes, we offer complete customization: dimensions, carving complexity, polish finishes (matte, glossy, antique or deco) and fabric upholstery according to your interior requirements.
